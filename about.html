Company

Who we are

WeMake, Corp. is an innovative technology company headquartered in San Diego, California. Our slogan captures the work: Define techno-human convergence.

We build at the frontier where carbon-based life and silicon-based systems meet, and where human intelligence and artificial intelligence learn to work as partners. Our portfolio spans cognitive and motor health applications, cultural projects that preserve care and fun, clinical research capabilities, regenerative collaborations, and platforms that help institutions navigate a world shaped by biology and computation.

Our people include scientists, clinicians, engineers, and operators who have shipped products, run trials, and partnered across life sciences, neurotechnology, AI, and frontier engineering.

Mission

Why WeMake exists

Mission

Redefine how life and technology meet

To design, develop, and deploy technologies at the meeting point of living systems and computational systems, so that human intelligence and artificial intelligence strengthen each other for healthier, more capable, more joyful lives.

Vision

One continuum of capability and care

A future in which carbon and silicon, mind and machine, operate as a continuum: biology informed by data, machines guided by human judgment, and innovation accountable to people, communities, and the fun that makes us human.

Principles

How we work

01

Convergence first

We prefer problems that sit on both sides of the carbon-silicon line, or that demand human and machine intelligence together. That is our home terrain.

02

Human in the loop

AI is a partner, not a replacement for care, consent, clinical judgment, or the care and fun that hold communities together.

03

From lab to life

We respect science and regulation as much as product craft. Breakthroughs matter when they deploy safely, clearly, and at a human scale.
